How to use the dashboard

1. Open a suite

Click Available suites, then click the suite you want to change. Each row shows the unit number, type and rent.

2. Change what you need

Update the rent, square feet, move-in date, or switch Available now on or off. Add photos with the image boxes. To remove a rented suite, click the ✕ on its row.

3. Save

Click Save at the top left. This stores your change as a draft. Nothing on the public website has changed yet.

4. Check the preview

Use the Published / In review menu at the top to open the preview link. It shows the website with your change, so you can be sure it looks right.

5. Publish

When you're happy, click Publish. The website updates in about a minute. If several suites change in the same week, save them all first and publish once.

Adding a suite: click Add suite at the top of the list. The new blank suite appears at the bottom of the list and the page scrolls to it automatically. Fill it in, then Save and Publish.
Need something changed that isn't here? Text or email Abdullah — layout, wording and new sections are handled outside this dashboard.
